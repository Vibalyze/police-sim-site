Support

Siren City for iOS and Android

Write to mert@maindtec.de. It is a small studio, so a real person answers.

Reporting a bug

Tell us your phone model and OS version, your rank, and what you had just tapped. A screenshot helps a lot.

Restoring Pro

Open Settings › Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Ads

Rewarded ads only play when you tap to watch one. Occasional ads between shifts are never shown during a call and never to Pro owners. To change your ad privacy choices, open Settings › Privacy options.

My progress is gone

Your save lives on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it unless your phone's backup restores it. There is no cloud save yet.

Offline earnings

Patrols earn while you are away, up to a cap shown on screen (4 hours at first, more with station upgrades, 12 hours with Pro).

Content and age rating

Siren City is rated 9+: cartoon police work, no blood, arrests are non-lethal. If a call or line feels wrong, tell us which and we will change it.
