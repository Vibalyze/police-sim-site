Privacy policy

Siren City for iOS and Android · Last updated 5 November 2026

Siren City has no account and no servers of its own. Your game is saved on your phone. The only data that leaves the device goes to Google's ad service (AdMob), which shows the optional and occasional ads, and to Apple or Google when you buy something. Both are explained below.

Who we are

Siren City is made and published by Vibalyze OÜ, Järvevana tee 9, 11314 Tallinn, Estonia (registry code 17240903). Contact: mert@maindtec.de.

What stays on your device

Your career is saved as files inside the app's own private storage: the game's random seed, the actions you took, your station, vehicles, rank and settings, and when the game was last open (so patrols can earn while you are away). Nothing in these files identifies you, and we never receive them. They are included in your phone's normal backup (iCloud or device backup on iOS, Auto Backup on Android), which Apple and Google handle under their own policies.

There is no login, no profile, no chat, no friends list and no online leaderboard. The callers, officers and suspects in the game are invented by the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one (for example to double a shift reward).
  • Occasional full-screen ads between shifts, never during a call, never in your first sessions, and never if you own Pro.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes data such as your IP address, your Android advertising ID (on Android, where your consent allows it), device and app information and ad interactions. Google acts as an independent controller for this data; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Your consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can accept, reject or choose purposes. Rejecting still lets you play; ads are then limited and not personalised.
  • iOS: the app never asks for tracking permission and never reads your advertising identifier (IDFA). Ads on iPhone and iPad are always requested as non-personalised.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options inside the game.

Purchases

Purchases (Pro, cosmetics) are processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive the store's confirmation that a product is owned, which the app keeps on the device so it can restore your purchase. Purchases are behind a parental gate.

The rating prompt

After a promotion the game may ask the operating system to show its standard rating sheet, a few times at most. That sheet is run by Apple or Google.

Analytics and crash reports

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service of ours. Apple and Google may provide us aggregated, anonymous statistics (installs, crashes) if you have agreed to share them with developers in your phone's settings.

Children

Siren City is a general-audience game rated 9+. It is not directed primarily at children and is not in the Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are served with Google's family-friendly content settings, and purchases need a parental gate. If you believe a child's data was processed, contact us and we will help you reach Google to have it removed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and object. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests concern Google's ad data: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings and change consent in the game. Deleting the app deletes the save. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee) or your local authority.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page is updated before that version ships.
