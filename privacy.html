Privacy policy

Siren City for iOS and Android · Last updated 5 November 2026 (version 1.0: no ads)

Siren City has no account and no servers of its own. Your game is saved on your phone. We collect no data about you. The only time anything leaves the device is when you buy something through Apple or Google, explained below.

Who we are

Siren City is made and published by Vibalyze OÜ, Järvevana tee 9, 11314 Tallinn, Estonia (registry code 17240903). Contact: mert@maindtec.de.

What stays on your device

Your career is saved as files inside the app's own private storage: the game's random seed, the actions you took, your station, vehicles, rank and settings, and when the game was last open (so patrols can earn while you are away). Nothing in these files identifies you, and we never receive them. They are included in your phone's normal backup (iCloud or device backup on iOS, Auto Backup on Android), which Apple and Google handle under their own policies.

There is no login, no profile, no chat, no friends list and no online leaderboard. The callers, officers and suspects in the game are invented by the game.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Siren City shows no ads of any kind and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. It never asks for tracking permission and never reads your advertising identifier.

Purchases

Purchases (Pro, cosmetics) are processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive the store's confirmation that a product is owned, which the app keeps on the device so it can restore your purchase. Purchases are behind a parental gate.

The rating prompt

After a promotion the game may ask the operating system to show its standard rating sheet, a few times at most. That sheet is run by Apple or Google.

Analytics and crash reports

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service of ours. Apple and Google may provide us aggregated, anonymous statistics (installs, crashes) if you have agreed to share them with developers in your phone's settings.

Children

Siren City is a general-audience game (App Store 12+, for mild references such as a drunk-driver call). It is not directed primarily at children and is not in the Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. There are no ads, and purchases need a parental gate.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and object. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to hand over or delete; deleting the app deletes the save. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee) or your local authority.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page is updated before that version ships.
