Siren City

A police chief game for iPhone and Android

You are the new chief of a run-down precinct. Every emergency call pops up as a card: pick the right units, hit dispatch, and watch the result land. Upgrade your station, collect vehicles and rise from Cadet to Commander.

What you do

  • Dispatch. Send the right mix of police and ambulance to every call.
  • Act. Short traffic stops, chases where traffic pulls aside for your siren, and ambulance runs.
  • Build. Upgrade rooms, collect vehicles from blueprints, open new divisions.
  • Come back. Patrols earn while you are away, plus daily missions and a streak.

Fair by design

No energy, no gems, no loot boxes. Odds are the same for everyone and nothing you buy changes the outcome of a call. No ads at all. One optional Pro upgrade speeds up your patrols' earnings.
